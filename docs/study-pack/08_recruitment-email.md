# 08 — Recruitment email (Stage 1)

Recruitment material for the COMPGame Stage-1 focus-group study. Used **before** `01` (consent).
Fill every `[…]` placeholder before sending — see the checklist at the bottom.

> **Target:** 30 participants who *complete* the session. Because a ~60–90 min voluntary session
> has no-shows and dropout, **invite ~50–60** to seat 30.

---

## C. Before you send — required checks

1. **Ethics approval first.** PolyU requires human-subjects ethics review for studies with
   participants. FYP studies are usually covered under your supervisor's or the department's ethics
   process (e.g. the Human Subjects Ethics Sub-Committee). **Confirm you have clearance (or written
   sign-off from your supervisor) before recruiting**, and put the reference in the email. Recruiting
   before approval is the one genuinely irreversible mistake here.

2. **You cannot mass-email the whole student body.** Realistic channels, in order of yield:
   - Ask your **supervisor or a course lecturer to forward** version A to a class/cohort (highest trust, best response).
   - Post version B in **programme / society / course group chats** you're already in.
   - **Your own network** — classmates, then snowball ("forward to a friend").
   - A departmental noticeboard / student-union channel if one exists.
   - **Do not** email a scraped or exported address list — that risks the HK Personal Data (Privacy)
     Ordinance and spam rules. If you email individuals directly, use **BCC** so addresses aren't exposed.

3. **Oversample.** Invite ~50–60 to seat 30 completed (see target note above).

4. **Pilot before the full run.** Do a P00 self-run + 2–3 pilots (or ring-fence the first ~5 sign-ups)
   and fix any confusing item wording *before* opening to the rest — data already collected can't be re-worded.

5. **Placeholders to fill:** `[Name]` · `[programme]` · `[contact email]` · `[supervisor + department]` ·
   `[ethics reference]` · `[date(s) / location / online]` · `[duration]` · `[incentive]` · `[sign-up link]`.
